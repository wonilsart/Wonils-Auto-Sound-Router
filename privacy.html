Privacy Policy

Wonils Auto Sound Router · Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: this app collects nothing. No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no ads, and no personal data ever leaves your computer.

What the app does with data

Wonils Auto Sound Router is a Windows utility that manages your audio devices locally. Your settings (scenes, device preferences, colors, language, soundboard file paths) are stored in a local configuration file under %APPDATA%\SoundConfig on your own computer. They are never transmitted anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No collection of personal information of any kind
  • No telemetry, analytics, or usage tracking
  • No account registration or sign-in
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs
  • No network communication 𓅪 the app works fully offline

Microphone access

The app reads microphone levels locally to show live meters, to run acoustic pairing, and to power optional features such as auto mic switch and the voice changer. All audio processing happens in real time on your machine. Audio is never recorded to disk except when you explicitly press Record in the soundboard, and those recordings stay in your local sound library until you delete them.

Steam

If you installed the app through Steam, Valve's own services handle the download, updates, and any Steam community features. Those are governed by the Steam Privacy Agreement. The app itself does not use Steamworks APIs to collect or transmit data.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page and the app's store page will be updated before that version ships.
